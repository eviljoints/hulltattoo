## Follow the instructions for your own tattoo

Ask your artist for instructions that match your tattoo and dressing. Different coverings have different removal and replacement instructions: do not apply a general timetable from another person’s tattoo.

Our [aftercare page](/aftercare/) is the starting point for studio advice. If the instructions are unclear, contact the studio before changing the dressing or using a new product.

## Keep the routine simple

Wash and dry your hands before touching the area. Follow your artist’s directions for cleaning, drying, dressing and any recommended product. Do not pick at peeling skin or scabs, and ask your artist about activities or clothing that could rub the area.

There is no single healing deadline that applies to every tattoo. Avoid treating a photograph or a fixed number of days as proof that your tattoo is fully healed.

## Ask about products and dressings

Tell your artist if you have reacted to adhesives or skincare products before. Ask which product to use, how much to apply and when to stop. Do not start an antibiotic cream unless a medical professional recommends it.

## When to seek medical advice

Increasing redness, swelling or pain, or unusual discharge can be signs of a problem. Seek medical advice promptly if you suspect infection or an allergic reaction; do not wait for a social-media reply from the studio. An artist cannot diagnose or treat a medical condition.

If you have a health condition or take medication that may affect the procedure or healing, speak with your clinician before booking. Do not stop prescribed medication on the basis of tattoo advice.

## Further reading

[Torbay Council’s tattoo aftercare guidance](https://www.torbay.gov.uk/business/licensing/skin-piercing-acupuncture-and-tattooing/tattoo-aftercare/) explains hygiene and warning signs. Bring questions about your own tattoo to your artist and medical concerns to a qualified healthcare professional.
