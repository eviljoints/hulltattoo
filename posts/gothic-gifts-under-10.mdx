The [Black Magic gothic metal keyrings](https://noirnexus.myshopify.com/products/black-magic-gothic-keyrings?utm_source=hulltattoostudio&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=gothic_gift_guide) come in triple moon, pentagram, talking-board planchette and coffin designs. ## Make a small gift feel considered

Choose around the person's interests first: reading, cats, botanical illustration or a daily coffee ritual. One well-chosen object is enough; a handwritten note can make it feel more personal than several unrelated extras.
