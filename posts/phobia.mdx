## Tell the artist before booking

Feeling nervous about a tattoo is a reason to ask questions, not something you need to hide. Tell the artist what worries you: the sight of needles, pain, feeling faint, being unable to pause, or an earlier experience.

Tattooing and medical injections are different experiences, but one is not guaranteed to feel easier than the other. We cannot promise a painless appointment or that a tattoo will resolve a phobia.

## Questions worth asking

- Can you explain what will happen before starting?
- How long is the proposed session, and how do we agree a pause?
- What position will I be in, and can I avoid watching the equipment?
- Can we discuss the design and placement before I commit to a time slot?

Use the contact form below or your artist’s linked social profile to discuss these points. Explore [Mike](/mike/), [Harley](/harley/) and [Jen](/jen/) to compare their work before choosing an artist.

## Make a plan you feel comfortable with

Agree how you will communicate if you need a break. Ask whether headphones or another distraction are suitable for the appointment. Do not assume that a larger design or a longer session is the right starting point for you.

If you feel unwell or no longer want to proceed, tell the artist. You can choose to postpone rather than push through an appointment you do not feel ready for.

## When the concern needs more support

If fear of needles is affecting medical care or everyday life, speak with a healthcare professional. Tattoo artists can explain their process, but they do not provide treatment for anxiety or phobias.

When you feel ready, read [how booking works](/blog/Booking/) and discuss the appointment length before choosing an [available slot](/book/).
