## Before selecting an appointment

Start with the artist’s portfolio: [Mike](/mike/) works in realism, neo traditional and cover-ups; [Harley](/harley/) is developing dotwork and pointillism; [Jen](/jen/) is developing full-colour neo traditional work. Look for examples close to the style you have in mind.

Contact your chosen artist before booking if you are unsure how much time to reserve. Include the idea, approximate size in centimetres, body placement and clear reference images. For a cover-up, include a well-lit photograph of the existing tattoo.

## Book online through Wix

1. Open the [booking page](/book/) or the booking section on an artist’s profile.
2. Choose your artist and one of their assigned services.
3. Select an available date and appointment time. Times are shown in **Europe/London** time.
4. Review your selection, then continue to Wix checkout.
5. Check the final service, price and payment terms in Wix before paying.

A selected slot is not a confirmed booking. Returning to our website from checkout does not prove that payment completed. Look for your Wix booking confirmation and contact the studio if anything is unclear before trying again.

## Enquire with reference images

Use **Contact Us** on the homepage or below this article to describe your idea. You can attach up to 10 reference images, up to 5 MB each. Upload only images you have permission to share, and avoid including unnecessary personal information.

You can also contact the studio or artist through their linked social profiles, call **07940080790**, or visit **255 Hedon Road, Hull, HU9 1NQ**. Check availability before making a special trip.

## Prices, deposits and changes

The payment options and amount due are shown in Wix checkout. Discuss any quote or deposit arranged directly with the artist, and read the [terms](/terms/) before committing. Contact the studio as early as possible about changes or cancellations.

## Prepare for your visit

Bring the agreed reference material and wear clothing that gives access to the placement. Tell the artist about any concerns before the appointment. Read the [FAQ](/#faq-section) and ask which [aftercare instructions](/aftercare/) apply to your tattoo and dressing.
